Why me / मेरा जवाब

I love building things. It makes me feel alive. I like building simple systems that let everyone involved sleep peacefully at night.

I love shipping features people actually use. Seeing a usability metric move because of something I built makes my day. I enjoy getting on calls with customers, understanding what they want and why, then turning that into a prototype instead of a long design document.

I shine in early-stage products: I’ll use a jugaad when it gets us to the right learning, then solidify it into something precise and reliable. I’m drawn to systems engineering, inference, making things faster, and making them more dependable.

I’m curious beyond computer science, learn requirements quickly, and act on them. The application layer will keep changing; I want to be the person who can understand the problem, make a useful first version, and keep improving it when reality arrives.
